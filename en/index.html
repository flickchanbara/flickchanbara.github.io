HOW TO PLAY

Strike, block, collect.

斬

Slash 8 ways

On your turn, flick up, down, left, right or diagonally.

受

Read the swing

On your rival's turn, flick across the swing to block. A master teaches you in a short lesson.

藁

Straw cutting

Slash leaning straw posts across the grain, as fast as you can.

対

vs CPU and Versus

Easy to Oni. Duel friends nearby or anyone on Game Center; use a passcode to meet friends.

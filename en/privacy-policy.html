Privacy Policy

iPhone app "Flick Chanbara" (フリックチャンバラ)

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Flick Chanbara" (the "App") handles information. The Japanese version is the authoritative one.

1. Information the App itself collects

The App has no accounts or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. Your koban, items, records, titles, name and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the App deletes them.

2. Information sent to your opponent

In versus matches, your chosen name, the fighter, weapon and gear you use, and your match inputs and progress are sent directly to your opponent's device. They never pass through our servers and we never receive them. Nearby matches use Apple's peer-to-peer networking (Wi-Fi or Bluetooth); a passcode is never sent as is, only a short hash of it. Game Center matches use Apple's Game Center, which handles your Game Center profile under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

3. Information sent for advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). The Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK may send Google device identifiers (the IDFA only if you allow tracking), consent status, approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ads shown and your interactions with them, app usage, device type, OS version and crash information. We do not receive this information; we only see aggregated figures such as impressions and revenue. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. News

The App downloads a news file from our site on GitHub Pages (https://flickchanbara.github.io/). As part of normal networking, GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address and device information. We do not receive it.

5. Tracking permission

After the first lesson, when you reach the home screen, the App asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency whether you allow tracking with the IDFA. Every feature works the same if you decline; ads just won't be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. Koban, gacha and purchases

Koban are earned in the game (winning, cutting straw, watching video ads). They cannot be bought or exchanged for money. The gacha uses koban only, and its odds are shown in the App's settings.

The App offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Payment is handled by Apple's App Store; we never receive your payment details. If you buy it, the App no longer starts the advertising SDKs and sends nothing for advertising.

7. Children

The App is suitable for a wide audience but is not made specifically for children. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page. Contact: penguinnonawabari@gmail.com
